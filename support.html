Frequently asked questions

Search setup and troubleshooting answers.

How do I sync my data across devices?

Sign into the same Apple ID on all devices and enable iCloud for Touchline Studio in Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud.

How do I import my roster?

Open your season roster and use Import. CSV and Excel are supported, with a preview and column mapping step before save.

How does the AI Drill Creator work?

It uses OpenRouter and needs your own API key. The app can be used fully without enabling this feature.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Core planning and session features work offline. Internet is only required for AI drill generation.

How do I take notes during a session?

Open a session and run it in live mode, then tap a player to attach quick notes or dictation while practice is active.

Can I share drills with other coaches?

Direct sharing is on the roadmap. Today, you can share drill details via screenshots and exported notes.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items in-app, uninstall the app to clear local data, and manage iCloud storage separately for synced data removal.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes. Touchline Studio runs on Mac and syncs via iCloud with your iPhone and iPad.
