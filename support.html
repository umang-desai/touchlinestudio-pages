Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my data across devices?
Touchline Studio uses iCloud to sync your data. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID on all devices and that iCloud is enabled for Touchline Studio in Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Apps Using iCloud.
How do I import my roster?
Go to your season's Roster tab and tap the import button. You can import from CSV or Excel files. The app will show you a preview and let you map columns before importing. Make sure your file has columns for player names, and optionally jersey numbers and positions.
How does the AI Drill Creator work?
The AI Drill Creator uses OpenRouter to generate drill suggestions based on your parameters (goal, duration, skill level, equipment, etc.). You'll need to provide your own OpenRouter API key in Settings. The feature is optional—the app works fully without it.
Can I use the app offline?
Yes! All core features work offline. Your data is stored locally on your device. iCloud sync happens automatically when you're back online. The only feature requiring internet is the AI Drill Creator.
How do I take notes during a session?
Open your session and tap "Run Session" to enter live mode. You'll see your players listed—tap any player to add a note. You can type or use the microphone for voice dictation. Notes are automatically linked to the session and player.
Can I share drills with other coaches?
Currently, drills are stored in your personal library. Export/sharing features are on our roadmap. In the meantime, you can describe your drills to other coaches or take screenshots of the drill details and diagrams.
How do I delete my data?
You can delete individual items (teams, seasons, drills, etc.) within the app. To delete all data, you can delete the app from your device. If you use iCloud sync, also go to Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Touchline Studio to remove cloud data.
Is there a Mac version?
Yes! Touchline Studio is available on Mac through the Mac App Store. Your data syncs seamlessly between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac via iCloud.
