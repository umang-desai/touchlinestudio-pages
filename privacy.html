Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 18, 2026

Overview

Touchline Studio ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind. We believe your coaching data belongs to you, and we've built the app to minimize data collection while maximizing functionality.

Data Storage

Local Storage: All your data—teams, seasons, players, drills, sessions, and notes—is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync.

iCloud Sync: If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is:

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Accessible only by you through your Apple ID
  • Not accessible to Touchline Studio or any third parties
  • Subject to Apple's privacy policies

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device and your private iCloud account. We have no servers, no user accounts, and no way to access your coaching data.

AI Features

The AI Drill Creator feature uses OpenRouter to generate drill suggestions. When you use this feature:

  • Only the drill parameters you provide (goal, duration, intensity, etc.) are sent to the AI service
  • No player names, personal information, or other coaching data is transmitted
  • You provide your own API key, which is stored securely on your device
  • AI requests are subject to OpenRouter's privacy policy

The AI feature is entirely optional. The app is fully functional without it.

Analytics & Tracking

Touchline Studio does not include any analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party services that collect usage data. We do not track:

  • How you use the app
  • What features you access
  • How long you spend in the app
  • Any device identifiers

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Apple services:

  • iCloud (optional): For syncing data across your devices
  • Speech Recognition (optional): For voice-to-text note taking, processed on-device or via Apple's servers depending on your iOS version

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual items within the app
  • Deleting the app from your device (removes all local data)
  • Managing your iCloud storage through iOS Settings (removes synced data)

Children's Privacy

Touchline Studio is designed for coaches and does not knowingly collect information from children. Player information you enter (such as names and jersey numbers) is stored only on your device and your private iCloud account.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
